Web and AI product developer with over fourteen years of experience translating
business requirements into products that are straightforward to operate and
inexpensive to maintain. My work sits at the intersection of web engineering,
e-commerce and applied artificial intelligence: platforms, extensions and automation
systems built for production environments rather than demonstrations.

My practice covers the full delivery cycle — interface design, server-side
implementation, integration of external services, and the documentation that keeps a
system intelligible long after launch. I favour explicit architecture, clear module
boundaries and verifiable behaviour over expedient shortcuts, and I place particular
value on the discipline of turning an early concept into a defined, deliverable scope.

**Areas of practice**

- **E-commerce and extension engineering** — WordPress and WooCommerce platforms, custom plugins, editor and page-builder integrations
- **Applied AI** — language-model integration, agentic workflows, content and data automation
- **Systems and integration** — REST APIs, provider adapters, caching, rate control and data pipelines
- **Product engineering** — requirements, architecture, documentation and long-term maintainability

**Available for** product development engagements, custom plugin and platform work,
and AI integrations that address a concrete operational need.
